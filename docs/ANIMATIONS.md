# Clashy animations: audit and scorecard (Nov 25)

This covers every animation the app ships: the Lottie files (`app/src/ui/lottie.tsx`), the Reanimated game FX
(`app/src/ui/fx.tsx`), the clay video loops (`app/src/ui/clay.tsx`) and the result moments of the duel
(`app/src/app/onchain.tsx`) and the Royale (`app/src/app/onchain-royale.tsx`). I rendered each Lottie with lottie-web
(svg renderer, the same player the web build uses) frame by frame over the real clay result tiles, and read the
Reanimated code. Verdicts are **keep**, **improve** or **replace**.

## What changed in this pass

The result moment used to be one thing: confetti on a win, nothing at all on a loss, a liquidation or a dead heat.
It's now four pure-vector Lottie files, one per outcome, generated by `app/scripts/make-result-lottie.py`:

| File | Outcome | What it does | Size | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `result-win.json` | You won / You survived | A gold flash and shockwave come off the tile, then 18 spinning vector coins fountain out and fall. A gold rim glow blooms and slim light rays fan out (none point down at the title). Then a 2 s idle loop runs: the glow breathes, the rays sway and sparkles twinkle. | 54 KB | 2 s intro + 2 s loop |
| `result-lose.json` | You lost | The tile dims. A red rim contracts inward (the opposite of the win's shockwave) and settles low. Grey crumbs chip off the two bottom corners and drift outward, with soft dust. It holds on the dimmed frame. Nothing gory. | 39 KB | 1.8 s, holds |
| `result-liquidated.json` | Liquidated / Knocked out (Royale) | The KO hit: a white-to-red flash, a screen shake and a red starburst at the impact. Glass cracks draw out from the impact, a spider-web ring closes around it, and shards fly up and out. The cracks stay on the last frame. | 43 KB | 2 s, holds |
| `result-dead-heat.json` | Dead heat | Mirrored. A lavender orb (you) and a pink orb (rival) arrive from each side, and each one draws its half of the rim. White glints appear where the halves meet. Then a 2 s loop of pulses and ripples, matched on both sides. | 20 KB | 1.5 s intro + 2 s loop |

- Bodymovin 5.7.4, 60 fps, 300 × 300, transparent. The files hold no image assets and no stock content. Every
  path is computed with fixed seeds, so a re-run gives the same output.
- They sit in the same slot the confetti used (`size={300}`, `left: -65, top: -70` over the 170 px clay tile). The
  layout, text, colours, fonts and copy are unchanged. The clay video under them is unchanged.
- Particles are kept out of the title column under the tile. Coins fountain up and to the sides, crumbs leave from
  the corners and shards fly up and out, so nothing falls across "You won", "You lost" or "Liquidated".
- Colours come only from the brand: gold `#FFC94A` for wins, red `#FF453A` for losses, lavender `#AB9FF2` for you,
  pink `#F0468C` for the rival, plus white and greys.
- `lottie.tsx` now reads two markers. `loop` plays the intro once and then loops the idle segment. `still` is the
  frame shown under reduced motion: the win's glow, the dimmed loss, the cracked KO and the balanced rims. So
  reduced motion gets a still image of the outcome instead of a blank space.
- Wiring: in the duel the outcome picks `win`, `knockout` (liquidated), `deadHeat` or `lose`, mirroring the title's
  own logic. In the Royale a survivor gets `win` and a knocked-out player gets `knockout`. A spectator gets nothing,
  as before.
- How I checked it: I took contact sheets of every file over the real clay tiles, plus phone-width (375 px) mocks
  with the title, amount and button. A headless test of the player logic confirmed that win and dead heat enter
  their loop segment, that lose and KO hold their last frame, and that reduced motion lands on the `still` frame.
  `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` passes.

## Scorecard

### Lottie (`app/src/ui/lottie.tsx`)

| Animation | Used in | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `confetti.json` (old win confetti) | duel + Royale result (until now) | **Replace (done)** | Off-brand indigo `#5B6CFF` and mint, which aren't in the palette. The spray is lopsided, from below the centre. The pieces are flat rectangles with no flutter, and they repeat the confetti already painted in the win clay art. The pieces fall straight across the "You won" title. It only ever played on wins, so a loss, a KO or a dead heat had no moment at all. The file is no longer referenced by any screen; it stays in `FILES` and can be deleted. |
| `result-*.json` (new) | duel + Royale result | **Keep** | See above. |
| `heart-pop.json` | feed like, post like | **Keep** | Vector and 6 KB, crisp, and it reads instantly. Nits: two of its six dots are the off-brand indigo and mint, and the ring stroke is heavy at frame 8. A 10-minute fix if anyone touches it. |
| `coin-drop.json` | Stash save, "Saved to your Stash" row | **Improve** | The squash and bounce of the jar are good. But it embeds two PNG cutouts (76 KB), so it goes soft at 2×. The coin is tiny against the jar at 44 px, and the sparkles use the old indigo and mint. Rebuild the coin as the same vector coin `result-win.json` uses. |
| `flame.json` | inbox streak | **Improve (low)** | One raster PNG with a ±2° wobble and a 4% squash, so it reads as almost static. That's 27 KB for one image. A vector flame with a real flicker (path morph on three tongues) would be better and smaller. |
| `squad-war.json` | Arena tile, clan war card | **Improve (size)** | The motion is the best of the old set: a charge, a clash, the VS slam, a shake and a reset on a 3 s loop. But it's 216 KB of embedded raster cutouts, far over budget for a looping tile. Lower-res cutouts (or vector crests) would bring it under 80 KB. |

### Reanimated FX (`app/src/ui/fx.tsx`)

These run on Reanimated 4, whose animations default to `ReduceMotion.System`. Under reduced motion they jump to
the end state rather than playing.

| FX | Used in | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Hit` | duel KO marker (live), Royale lost-heart | **Keep** | A springy `ZoomIn` (damping 8) with a 0.9 s auto-hide reads well as a hit. Nit: no haptic. The live KO uses the raster `fx-ko` art, which fits the clay look. |
| `RoundBanner` | Royale rounds | **Keep** | A clean pop-in with a ribbon and an auto-hide. |
| `Bob` | lobby art | **Keep** | A gentle idle that does its job. |
| `Fill` | duel HP bars | **Keep** | 450 ms ease-out, and it turns red under 25%. |
| `WinBurst` | **unused** | **Replace** | It rotates and scales two raster sheets (coin burst and confetti) together, so it reads as a spinning sticker, not a burst. `result-win.json` does this job properly now. Delete it, or reimplement it on the Lottie. |
| `Countdown` | **unused** | **Keep (if used)** | A good 3-2-1-GO with a spring per number and haptics. Small bug: the effect fires a Heavy haptic again when `n` reaches −1, a double thump after GO. Move the haptic below the `n < 0` check. |
| `VsIntro` | **unused** | **Keep (if used)** | Solid structure: the sides slide in, VS slams down, then a shake. The 1.7 s timer calls `onDone` even if the view is gone, which is fine because it's cleared on unmount. |
| `Shake` | **unused** | **Keep** | A standard five-step shake that skips the first render. |
| `DangerPulse` | **unused** | **Improve before use** | A flat 10 px red border on the screen edge looks like a browser focus ring, not danger. Use an inner red vignette (radial gradient) instead. |
| `useFloaters` / `CountUp` (fx) | **unused** | **Keep** | They're fine. The games kit has its own `CountUp`. |

## Phones (added Nov 25)

`app/src/ui/lottie.native.tsx` plays the same JSON files and markers with `lottie-react-native` (~7.5.0, the version
`expo install` picks for this Expo SDK): the intro once, then the `loop` segment; the `still` frame under reduced motion.
The JSON list is shared in `app/src/ui/lottie-files.ts`. The squad war scene now uses the Lottie on phones too, not the
still tile. Checked: the iOS JS bundle includes the native player and not lottie-web, and the web bundle includes lottie-web and
not the native player. On Nov 25 a native debug build (`npx expo run:ios`, Xcode 26.5, iPhone 17 simulator) played the
squad war Lottie on the Play tab: the crests slide in and the VS burst lands, then it loops.

## Sprites: the mascot and LASER (added Nov 25)

Twelve clay sprite animations, six per character, made with ChatGPT's image model from the approved mascot sheet and
the LASER film still (one 3 × 2 sheet of six frames per animation, same chat so the characters stay on model):

| Mascot (you) | LASER (the rival) |
|---|---|
| `bo-idle` fight stance (loop) | `laser-idle` trash talk into the megaphone (loop) |
| `bo-punch` | `laser-attack` laser eyes |
| `bo-hit` | `laser-hit` |
| `bo-ko` falls, X eyes, circling stars | `laser-ko` falls, glasses fly off |
| `bo-win` victory jump (loop) | `laser-win` gloats, coins (loop) |
| `bo-lose` | `laser-lose` |

- **Cut:** `python3 -I app/scripts/make-sprites.py` slices each sheet, removes the white background with an edge flood
  fill (the cream belly stays), sizes every sheet of a character by the area of its body colour so all its animations
  match, and lines every frame up on the feet. Output: `app/assets/sprites/<name>.webp` (one row of frames, 971 KB for
  all twelve), `sprites.json` (frames, frame size, fps, loop mode) and animated previews in `assets/sprites/preview/`.
  The ChatGPT originals stay on disk in `assets/sprites/src/` (12 MB, not in git).
- **Player:** `app/src/ui/sprite.tsx` steps through a strip on web and on phones. One-shots hold their last frame, and
  reduced motion shows a still frame.
- **Where they play:** `app/src/ui/fight.tsx`, the fight stage at the top of every live clash's board (on-chain and
  practice). It only acts out the real scores: when the lead swings to you, the mascot punches and LASER takes the hit,
  and the other way round. At the bell the leader celebrates and the other loses; a liquidation is a knockout.
- **Review page:** `/sprites` plays all twelve and has a fight stage driven by buttons. Checked on the iOS simulator
  (native build) on Nov 25: all twelve play; "You gain" then "Bell" ends with the mascot's victory jump and LASER
  slumped.
