# Clashy 3D object kit

One premium object per image, made with ChatGPT's image model. The set is shared by the website and the mobile app,
and matches the approved app comp v4 (night navy, indigo for you, pink for the opponent).

- **Material:** polished chrome (mirror steel with crisp reflections) paired with deep glossy enamel in indigo and
  pink, like a premium esports trophy or a lacquered collectible. Clean, sharp bevels; heavy, expensive-looking weight.
- **Palette:** night navy `#07071A` (shadows and reflections), indigo `#5B6CFF` (main enamel, "you"), hot pink
  `#F0468C` (accent, "the opponent"), with a cool lavender `#ECEDFB` rim light. Up `#4ADE9A` / down `#FF6B7A` appear
  only on price lines.
- **Face:** none on the hero, trophy and crests (a duel game reads better without a toy face). Only the empty-state
  objects get Clashy's mark: two short glowing indigo visor-slit eyes.
- **Background:** transparent for every object (they sit on `#07071A` and `#10112A` cards); seamless night-navy
  studio sweep with an indigo-to-pink horizon glow for the hero scene, splash, onboarding and OG card.
- **Subjects:** crossed swords (the duel), a knockout line, a stopwatch (60 s rounds), a market cube, a pencil drawing
  a price line, a clan shield, a war flag, a trophy and a crown.
- **Banned:** text, letters, numbers, real logos or brands (no Solana or MagicBlock marks), Supercell/Clash of Clans
  visuals, fantasy medieval kitsch, faces on weapons.

Style line appended to every prompt:
> premium 3D render, polished mirror chrome with deep glossy indigo #5B6CFF enamel and hot pink #F0468C accents,
> soft studio lighting, cool lavender rim light, crisp reflections of a dark navy room, clean sharp bevels,
> collectible esports-trophy quality, centred single object, transparent background (no background, no ground
> shadow), no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.
